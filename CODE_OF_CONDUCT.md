# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free environment for contributors and users.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, design, and evidence.
- Assume good faith while asking for clarification.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory or sexualised language;
- deliberate disruption or spam;
- publishing another person's private information;
- coordinated manipulation of repository metrics or discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when this policy is violated.
